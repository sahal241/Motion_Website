GOOD TO KNOW

Curious minds.
Happy tails.

Every dog is a little different. Here are a few answers to help you feel ready.

Plan a stay ↗
What will my dog eat?+

Bring their usual food and feeding instructions so their routine feels familiar. Let us know about any sensitivities or special meal needs in the booking notes.

What should I pack?+

Their food, any care instructions, and a favorite blanket or toy are a lovely start. A familiar scent can make a new room feel like home.

Is it okay if this is their first stay away?+

Of course. Tell us what helps them settle, and we'll keep their introduction gentle. Short visits and familiar comforts can help first-time guests find their paws.

Can you follow medication or care routines?+

Add the details to your stay notes so the team can review them with you before arrival. We'll confirm what your dog needs and whether we can support their routine.

Will I hear how they're doing?+

We know you miss them too. Share your preferred way to hear from us when arranging a real stay, and we'll talk through updates together.
